PCB Wiring Reference

Current, PCB-design-stage pin assignment reference for the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (U1). Matches the KiCad schematic as of 2026-09-19, after pins were reassigned during routing for cleaner trace layout. Supersedes the breadboard-era "Quick Wiring Reference."

Locked vs. Flexible Pins

  • Hard-locked (silicon-level, cannot move): USB D+/D- — GPIO19/20 are the only pins with native USB hardware. SPI uses the dedicated FSPI peripheral pins for performance, though other GPIOs could technically bit-bang it if ever forced to move.
  • Soft-assigned (any free GPIO works electrically): Buttons, sense pins, IMU interrupts, Power LED, Status LED. These were reshuffled during layout for cleaner routing.

Power/Charging Sense — flexible

ESP32-S3 PinModule pinSignalTypeNotes
GPIO3528V_BATT_SEN (battery voltage sense)Analog100k/100k divider off +BATT (pre-latch, always reads true battery voltage regardless of VBAT/VBUS jumper position). 100nF at the sense node. See Battery Voltage Sense doc.
GPIO1710VBUS_SENDigital in33k/47k divider off VBUS. Detects USB plugged in/out.
GPIO1811CH_STATDigital in10k/20k divider off MCP73831 STAT. Charging vs. complete (only meaningful when VBUS_SEN is high).

Combine VBUS_SEN + CH_STAT in firmware for full 3-state charge status: unplugged / charging / complete.

Power Latch & Status

ESP32-S3 PinModule pinSignalNotes
GPIO3932PWR_BTNPower button. Also does double duty as latch-hold: firmware drives this high after boot to hold the power latch on (see Power Latch Circuit doc). Requires a minimum button-hold-time check in firmware before latching, to avoid a boot-race if released too early.
GPIO4033PWR_LED220Ω resistor, GPIO drives high to light. Firmware-controlled "booted and running" indicator, distinct from the hardware-driven charge-status LED (D1/STAT), since this one only lights once firmware has finished booting and latched power on.
GPIO4134STATUS_LEDGeneral-purpose status/debug LED, GPIO-driven. Meaning open to firmware (e.g. BLE connection state, error/fault indication, activity blink).

Boot/Reset Jumpers — locked (breakout for existing circuit)

NetModule pinJumperNotes
ESP_BOOT (GPIO0)27J12-pin, 0.1" header, other leg to GND. Bridge to force download mode. 10k pull-up (R5). Hand-solder only, DNP for assembly, remove after bring-up.
EN3J210k pull-up with 1µF cap (C4) for reset delay. Bridge to force reset.

Buttons — flexible

Part: GCT SWT0210-045026GSA, 4.5x4.5mm gull-wing SMD, 260gf, 100k cycles. Debounce: firmware-only (74HC14 Schmitt circuit dropped for space/cost reasons) — direct GPIO-to-switch connection using ESP32-S3's internal 45kΩ pull-up, plus a light 22nF cap per button for fast-noise filtering. Firmware handles bounce timing (~10-20ms ignore window). 10 buttons total (A, B, D-pad x4, +, -, Home, Power).

Unconnected Pins (no-connect flags in schematic)

  • GPIO1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 15, 16, 42 — general-purpose pins with no assignment on this revision.
  • GPIO43, 44 (TXD0/RXD0) — default UART0 pins, left unconnected. Native USB (GPIO19/20) is the serial/programming path, plus the J1 download-mode jumper.
  • GPIO36, 37 — internal PSRAM/flash (N16R8), left unconnected.
